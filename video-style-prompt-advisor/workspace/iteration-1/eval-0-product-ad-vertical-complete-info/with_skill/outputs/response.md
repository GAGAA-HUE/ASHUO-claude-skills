# 视频风格参考方案

## 项目理解
15 秒竖屏无线耳机广告，投放抖音 + 小红书，主打降噪 + 佩戴舒适，目标用户 25-35 岁都市白领，预算有限，需先定视觉方向并产出 GPT image2 参考图。

---

## 方向一：通勤静音舱（降噪卖点优先）

**视觉逻辑**：把耳机变成一个"移动静音舱"。画面里人物身处嘈杂地铁/街头，但耳周被一圈柔和的降噪光晕包裹，外部噪声元素（声波、人群、车流）被半透明屏障隔开。强调"一戴上，世界静下来"。

**适合场景**：抖音前 3 秒强冲突钩子（嘈杂 vs 静谧对比），小红书封面用"静音舱"概念图。

**GPT Image 2 提示词**：
```
Vertical 9:16 product advertisement key visual. A young East Asian office worker (around 30, smart casual blazer, neutral tones) standing in a crowded noisy subway station during morning rush hour. Around her head, a translucent glowing soundproof bubble dome isolates her in calm silence — outside the dome, blurred commuters and motion streaks in cool gray-blue; inside the dome, warm soft light, serene expression, eyes closed slightly. She wears sleek minimalist white wireless earbuds. Visual metaphor of noise cancellation. Cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, high detail, photorealistic, color grade: cool exterior vs warm interior contrast. Negative space at top for headline text. No text in image.
```

---

## 方向二：云端轻盈感（佩戴舒适卖点优先）

**视觉逻辑**：用"悬浮/失重"隐喻佩戴无感。耳机像漂浮在耳畔的羽毛、云朵、气泡上，人物表情放松到像在打盹。强调"轻到忘记它的存在"。

**适合场景**：小红书调性更合，适合做"沉浸式舒适"的氛围图；抖音可做 0–3 秒的视觉锚点。

**GPT Image 2 提示词**：
```
Vertical 9:16 lifestyle product visual. Close-up profile of a relaxed young woman (late 20s, soft makeup, cream knit sweater) leaning back in a sunlit home office, eyes gently closed, serene half-smile. She wears tiny matte-white wireless earbuds. Around the earbuds, delicate floating elements — soft feathers, tiny clouds, light bubbles — drifting upward, conveying weightlessness and comfort. Morning window light, sheer curtains, plant in soft focus background. Airy, minimal, Japanese-Scandinavian aesthetic. Pastel cream and pale blue palette. Photorealistic with subtle surreal floating accents. Top area left clean for copy. No text.
```

---

## 方向三：分屏对比式（卖点直给，预算友好）

**视觉逻辑**：竖屏天然适合上下/左右分屏。一半是"没戴耳机"的焦躁通勤，一半是"戴上耳机"的从容办公/咖啡厅。强对比、信息密度高、制作成本低（可实拍 + 简单后期），适合预算紧张时优先验证。

**适合场景**：抖音信息流广告，3 秒内讲清卖点；小红书可做"前后对比"封面。

**GPT Image 2 提示词**：
```
Vertical 9:16 split-screen comparison ad key visual. Top half: same young male office worker (32, shirt sleeves rolled) in a noisy open office, hands on head, stressed, coworkers blurred behind, harsh fluorescent light, chaotic papers. Bottom half: same man now calm, leaning back with eyes half-closed, wearing minimalist white wireless earbuds, soft warm cafe lighting, coffee cup, tidy desk, peaceful expression. Clear visual divide between the two halves with a thin glowing horizontal line. Color contrast: top cool chaotic blue-gray, bottom warm calm amber. Photorealistic, commercial photography style, clean composition. Center band reserved for brand logo placement. No text.
```

---

## 方向四：极简产品英雄镜头（高级感 + 低预算）

**视觉逻辑**：不靠人物叙事，直接把产品放在干净背景里做"英雄镜头"，用光影和材质说话。降噪用"声波被吸收"的视觉符号（波纹消散），舒适用"贴合耳廓的柔光"表达。出图快、后期轻、调性高级，适合先做一版验证审美方向。

**适合场景**：小红书产品图/首图；抖音可作为结尾定帧 + logo 落版。

**GPT Image 2 提示词**：
```
Vertical 9:16 hero product shot. A pair of premium matte-white wireless earbuds resting on a smooth pale concrete surface, dramatic soft side lighting from left, long gentle shadow. Behind the earbuds, faint sound-wave ripples in the air dissolving into silence, visualized as fading concentric arcs. A single soft feather resting beside them to imply lightness. Minimalist studio background, gradient from warm white to soft gray. High-end commercial product photography, macro detail on earbud texture, shallow depth of field. Negative space in upper third for headline. No text, no watermark.
```

---

## 落地建议

1. **先出 4 张参考图**：用上面 4 个提示词各跑一张，横向对比哪个调性最贴合品牌，再收敛到 1–2 个方向深做。
2. **预算分配建议**：方向三（分屏对比）制作成本最低，可优先做完整 15 秒；方向一/二需要更多后期，可作为"升级版"备选。
3. **竖屏构图要点**：所有图都按 9:16 出，主体居中偏下，顶部留 1/3 给标题/品牌名，底部留安全区给抖音/小红书 UI 遮挡。
4. **15 秒节奏建议**：0–3 秒强冲突（嘈杂/压力）→ 3–10 秒产品介入 + 卖点可视化 → 10–15 秒情绪释放 + 品牌落版。
5. **平台差异**：抖音偏"前 3 秒抓人 + 卖点直给"，小红书偏"氛围感 + 封面美学"。同一产品可做两版开头，主体素材复用。
