# H3 专用规则参考文档（v2.0）

本文档基于官方 `h3-prompt-writing` skill 的 `references/base-en.txt` 与 `references/ref-en.txt` 整理，记录 Hailuo H3 提示词的完整格式规范、字段定义、标签语法、时间轴、音频控制、硬约束与技术限制。

---

## 1. 任务模式总览

H3 支持 **5 种生成模式**，由用户输入的素材类型决定：

| 模式 | 全称 | 中文 | 触发条件 | 首行指令 |
|------|------|------|---------|---------|
| **T2VA** | Text-to-Video-Audio | 文生视频 | 纯文字，无参考素材 | 无 |
| **I2VA** | Image-to-Video-Audio | 图生视频 | 用户提供首帧 | 有（见 §2.1） |
| **FL2VA** | First-Last-to-Video-Audio | 首末帧生视频 | 用户提供首帧+末帧 | 有（见 §2.2） |
| **L2VA** | Last-to-Video-Audio | 末帧生视频 | 用户仅提供末帧 | 有（见 §2.3） |
| **Ref2VA** | Full-Reference | 全参考模式 | 多素材综合复用（角色+视频+音频） | 见 §3 |

---

## 2. Base 模式（T2VA / I2VA / FL2VA / L2VA）

### 2.1 首行指令模板

#### T2VA（无首行指令）
直接进入三段式。

#### I2VA
```
For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.
```
（首行指令必须**完全照搬英文原文**，包括空格与大小写）

#### FL2VA
```
How the reference pictures align with the target video — Picture 1 (from Shot 1) aligns with the 0.00-second mark of the target video; Picture 2 (from Shot N) aligns with the S.SS-second mark of the target video.
```
- `N` 为最终 Shot 的真实编号
- `S.SS` 为有效视频时长（保留两位小数）

#### L2VA
```
How the reference pictures align with the target video — <Picture 1> (from [Shot N]) aligns with the S.SS-second mark of the target video.
```
- `<Picture 1>` 只属于最后一个 `[Shot N]`
- 描述需从前序合理状态推断到末帧

### 2.2 三段式核心字段

首行指令后空一行，然后按以下顺序写出三段：

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] ...
[Shot 2] At 00:03.500, the camera cuts to ...

overall_soundscape: ...

non_diegetic_music: ...
```

| 字段 | 描述内容 |
|------|---------|
| `integrated_multimodal_description` | 视觉、动作、镜头、说话人、对话、歌唱、与画面同步的 diegetic 音频 |
| `overall_soundscape` | 整段视频的环境音、动作音、非语言人声（1-4 句） |
| `non_diegetic_music` | 观众独享的配乐（角色听不到）（1-3 句） |

### 2.3 关键帧融入方法

#### I2VA：从首帧出发，向前延展

`<Picture 1>` 是 `[Shot 1]` 第 0.00 秒的真实首帧。先锚定图片中的风格、主体、构图、场景，再描述下一动作。角色身份、服装、颜色、关键道具、空间关系须保持一致。

**推荐结构**：first-frame anchor → action onset → continuous development → result or reaction

#### FL2VA：描述首末帧之间的路径

Picture 1 为开场，Picture 2 为结尾。聚焦主体的运动、姿势变化、物体变形、构图演化、场景/光线过渡。

FL2VA 通常偏好单镜头，让模型在首末帧之间连续插值；仅在显式需要多镜头时才使用多 Shot。最后一个 `[Shot N]` 必须落到末帧。

**推荐结构**：first-frame state → observable intermediate changes → progressively narrowing differences → last-frame state

#### L2VA：推断开场，落地到末帧

`<Picture 1>` 仅作为末帧锚点，属于最后一个 `[Shot N]`。需根据用户意图与末帧推断合理的前序状态，再描述角色、物体、镜头、场景如何逐渐逼近参考图。

**推荐结构**：plausible preceding state → explicit action and transition path → gradual convergence in the final shot → last-frame landing

## 3. Ref2VA 模式（Full-Reference）

### 3.1 整体结构（六段顺序）

| 段名 | 作用 |
|------|------|
| `subject_definitions` | 定义参考素材与参考标签 |
| `summary` | 概述任务类型、目标视频、主参考关系 |
| `retention_analysis` | 描述参考内容的保留/转移/复用关系 |
| `detailed_description` | 按时序描述视觉、动作、镜头、声音、对话（主字段） |
| `overall_soundscape` | 整段视频的环境音与物理音 |
| `non_diegetic_music` | 观众独享的配乐 |

### 3.2 参考标签系统

四种标签独立编号（同类内自增）：

| 标签 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| `<Subject N>` | 可见内容抽象单元（人物/动物/物体/场景/服装/界面/风格/动作） |
| `<Picture N>` | 关键帧/构图锚点/分镜参考 |
| `<Video N>` | 整段视频关系（编辑源/续写源/结构参考） |
| `<Audio N>` | 独立音频资产或参考视频的同步音轨 |

**一旦定义，所有段必须保持同一含义。**

`<Subject N>` 用法示例：
```text
<Subject 1> is the young woman in <Picture 1>, with long dark hair, a blue cardigan, and a thin silver necklace.
<Subject 1> is the woman whose appearance comes from <Picture 1> and whose walking motion comes from <Video 1>.
```

`<Picture N>` 单独使用场景：作为某 Shot 的首帧/末帧/构图锚点。仅作为角色/场景/服装定义来源时不创建单独 Picture 条目。

`<Video N>` 用于整段视频关系（编辑/续写/结构参考）。若视频中的元素作为可见内容复用，仍归 `<Subject N>`。

`<Audio N>` 与 `<Video N>` 独立编号。可来自同一参考视频但 `<Video 1>` 与 `<Audio 2>` 互不绑定。

### 3.3 `summary` 段：任务类型前缀

必须以方括号任务类型前缀开头，可组合（用 ` + `，不重复）：

| 前缀 | 适用场景 |
|------|---------|
| `[keyframe completion]` | 图像作为首帧/末帧/关键帧 |
| `[reference generation]` | 素材提供生成指导（不直接作为帧） |
| `[video editing]` | 直接修改源视频 |
| `[video continuation]` | 从源视频结尾继续 |
| `[audio reuse]` | 完整复用音频信号 |
| `[audio reference]` | 仅借用音频的音色/节奏/风格 |

视频编辑任务时，紧跟任务前缀写：
```text
The target video is an edited version of <Video 1>.
```

### 3.4 `retention_analysis` 段：关系标记

视觉内容（`<Subject N>` / `<Picture N>` / `<Video N>`）：
- `fully_preserved` — 完全保留
- `partially_preserved` — 部分保留
- `attribute_transfer` — 属性转移到其他可识别目标
- `weak_reference` — 仅保留风格/类别/氛围的宽泛相似

音频（`<Audio N>`）：
- `fully_copy` — 完整复制
- `partially_copy` — 部分复制
- `reference` — 仅借用风格/节奏/音色
- `weak_reference` — 仅保留宽泛相似

条目格式：
```text
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 3]): fully_preserved - ...
<Picture 2> ([Shot 1] first frame): fully_preserved - ...
<Audio 1>: reference - 仅借用 <Audio 1> 的音色，不复制原信号。
```

### 3.5 `detailed_description` 段

- **风格前置**：在 `[Shot 1]` 前用 1-2 句写全局风格声明
- **Shot 1 无时间戳**，从 Shot 2 起 `At MM:SS.mmm`
- **镜头运动**沿用 base 模式的三维度规范
- **Speaker ID** 沿用 `(S1)` / `(S2)` 编号规则
- **对话**用 `<d>[语言] 原文</d>` 标签
- **参考标签的引用时机**：
  ```text
  the shot begins from <Picture 1>
  the shot's keyframe corresponds to <Picture 2>
  the shot ends on <Picture 3>
  ```
- **长度参考**：生成任务通常 350-500 英文词；对话密集型优先完整台词覆盖

## 4. 三段写法细则

### 4.1 沿时间轴构建 multimodal description

每个细节都应对应可见/可听元素：视觉风格、初始构图、主体外观与位置、场景与关键道具、动作与反应、镜头变化、口语、与画面同步的 diegetic 声音。

`[Shot 1]` 开头先声明整体风格与初始构图。常见风格枚举：
`Cinematic` / `live-action` / `2D-animated` / `3D CG` / `claymation` / `watercolor` / `vintage film`

```text
[Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a medium-wide shot frames a baker opening the shutters of a small street bakery before sunrise.
```

### 4.2 切镜（Shots and Cuts）

- `[Shot 1]` 无时间戳
- 后续 Shot 用严格递增时间戳 `At MM:SS.mmm`
- 普通切镜用：`the camera cuts to` / `the shot cuts to` / `the shot transitions to` / `the shot changes to` / `the shot switches to`
- 用户明确要求时可用 cross-dissolve / fade / wipe
- 切镜应引入新信息（主体/空间/状态/视角/时间）；若仅距离或角度微变，优先用运镜而非切镜

### 4.3 镜头运动三维度

见 SKILL.md 主文件"镜头运动规范"章节。Motion type / Amplitude / Speed 三维组合，写成自然英文动作而非堆叠标签。

### 4.4 说话人 / 对话 / 歌唱

- 用 `(S1)`、`(S2)` 稳定编号
- 多人同时说话用复合 ID `(S1,S2)`
- 说话人在 `[Shot 1]` 首次发言时提供身份锚定（年龄/音色/语速/口音）
- 对话内容用 `<d>[语言] 原文</d>` 标签
- 对话原文逐字保留用户原句，不翻译不重写
- 旁白用 `says in an off-screen voiceover` 并注明 `while his lips remain completely closed`

跨镜头台词连续：
- 衔接点用 `<scenetrans>`
- 注明 `continues seamlessly across the cut` / `continues uninterrupted into the next shot` / `carries over from the previous shot` / `remains audible across the transition`

台词截断用 `<cutoff>`。

### 4.5 场景中可见的文字

任何招牌、霓虹灯、字幕、标签原文保留（用英文双引号）：

```text
A red neon sign reading "营业中" glows above the doorway.
```

### 4.6 `overall_soundscape`

- 1-4 句一段英文
- 描述环境音、动作音、非语言人声（风、雨、车流、脚步、布料、碰撞、呼吸、笑声、喘息）
- 不重复已写入 multimodal 的对话、歌唱、diegetic 音乐
- 完全静音用 `N/A`

### 4.7 `non_diegetic_music`

- 1-3 句
- 描述乐器、速度、节奏、动态变化
- 不用抽象情绪词、不解释情感功能
- diegetic 音乐（收音机/电视/手机/店内音乐）属于画面事件，写进 multimodal description
- 无配乐用 `N/A`

## 5. 技术约束（H3 平台硬限制）

| 约束 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **时长范围** | 4-15 秒（>15 秒必须走拼接协议） |
| **1080p 支持 15s** | 1080p 分辨率可支持最长 15 秒 |
| **512p 不支持末帧** | 512p 分辨率不支持设置 last frame（L2VA/FL2VA 末帧） |
| **提示词长度** | 无固定上限；生成任务正常参考 350-500 英文词，对话密集型以完整台词为准 |
| **镜头信息前置** | 三段式首字段为 `integrated_multimodal_description`，风格在 Shot 1 开头声明 |
| **避免堆叠多动作** | 一次性堆叠 5+ 动作会导致画面扭曲，按时间轴分段 |
| **商业文案逐字** | 包装/UI/品牌文字依赖提示词原文，改写或简化会导致文案错误 |

### 5.2 品牌资产真实性（品牌宣传场景）

当用户要做真实品牌、产品、官网或应用的宣传视频时：

- 不仿造或重绘现有品牌的 logo、wordmark、包装、UI、吉祥物或真实人物。
- 不编造产品功能、性能指标、口号、CTA、合规声明或市场数据。
- 身份承载素材（logo、包装、官方 UI、人物/吉祥物）须来自用户提供原件、官方页面/媒体包或已授权素材；无法验证时，请用户提供授权原件。
- 若仅用不明来源参考做概念设计，明确标注为非官方概念，不能暗示官方背书。
- 抽象图形、渐变、遮罩、粒子、过渡几何可生成，但不得冒充真实品牌识别资产。

### 5.3 模型版本

官方统一使用 **MiniMax H3**（无 3.0/2.3/Director 之分）。Director 模式在官方文档中未提及，不应作为模型分支出现。

## 6. >15 秒拼接协议

当目标时长超过 H3 单次生成上限时：

1. **锁定 Master Audio**：先确定完整音频作为全局音轨
2. **拆分镜头表**：总时长拆为多段 H3 短片（每段 2-5 秒）
3. **头尾帧衔接**：下一段首帧 = 上一段末帧（I2VA 模式）
4. **视觉锚链**：所有段共用同一全局风格声明、色调、镜头语言、角色逻辑
5. **剪辑拼接**：在剪辑软件中按 beat grid 对齐各段

## 7. 完整示例

### T2VA 示例（文生视频）

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a medium-wide shot frames a baker opening the shutters of a small street bakery before sunrise. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed as the middle-aged baker with a calm, slightly raspy voice (S1) places a fresh loaf on the wooden counter and says: <d>[English] First batch of the morning.</d> [Shot 2] At 00:05.000, the camera cuts to a close-up of steam rising from the sliced bread while the baker's final words carry over from the previous shot.

overall_soundscape: Wooden shutters scrape open over a quiet street as trays clink softly inside the bakery. The doorbell rings once, followed by light footsteps and the crisp sound of bread being sliced.

non_diegetic_music: A soft acoustic-guitar pattern at a moderate tempo, joined by sparse upright-bass notes and a gentle fade at the end.
```

### I2VA 示例（图生视频）

```text
For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, the young woman shown in <Picture 1> remains beside the rain-covered train window, preserving her appearance, clothing, seat position, and the carriage layout. The camera trucks right with small amplitude at slow speed as she lifts her gaze from the folded letter toward the passing city lights. Her reflection moves across the glass while the quiet, breathy young woman (S1) says: <d>[English] I get off at the next station.</d> She folds the letter along its existing crease.

overall_soundscape: The train wheels produce a steady metallic rhythm beneath a low ventilation hum. Rain ticks against the window while paper rustles softly in her hands.

non_diegetic_music: Sustained cello notes at a slow tempo with widely spaced piano tones, gradually decreasing in volume.
```

### FL2VA 示例（首末帧生视频）

```text
How the reference pictures align with the target video — Picture 1 (from Shot 1) aligns with the 0.00-second mark of the target video; Picture 2 (from Shot 1) aligns with the 8.00-second mark of the target video.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a rain-soaked cyclist begins in the position and framing established by Picture 1, holding a closed black umbrella beside a silver bicycle. The camera pulls out with small amplitude at slow speed as she releases the bicycle handle, raises the umbrella above her shoulder, and presses the runner upward until the canopy opens. Water rolls from the expanding fabric while she steps beneath it, rotates the handle into the final angle, and settles into the pose, spacing, and composition established by Picture 2 at the end of the shot.

overall_soundscape: Rain falls steadily on the pavement, followed by the metallic click of the umbrella runner and the soft snap of the canopy opening. Water drips from the bicycle frame as distant traffic passes.

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

### L2VA 示例（末帧生视频）

```text
How the reference pictures align with the target video — <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) aligns with the 6.00-second mark of the target video.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a close shot begins with an intact drinking glass near the edge of a dark wooden table, while the same hand and sleeve visible in <Picture 1> approach from the right. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed as the fingertips strike the rim. The glass tips, falls, and hits the floor with a sharp impact; cracks spread through it as fragments slide outward. Toward the end, the moving pieces lose momentum and settle into the exact broken arrangement, hand position, camera angle, lighting, and final composition established by <Picture 1>.

overall_soundscape: Fingertips tap the glass before it scrapes across the tabletop, falls, and breaks with a sharp crash. Small fragments scatter and gradually stop sliding across the floor.

non_diegetic_music: A low electronic pulse at a slow tempo, ending immediately after the glass breaks.
```
